## Design Context

### Users
College students, early-career professionals, hobbyists, lifelong learners, and freelancers looking to exchange skills and knowledge. They use SkillSwap to find local or online partners who can teach them something new in exchange for sharing their own expertise. They're motivated, curious, and value human connection over transactional learning.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, Trustworthy, Modern** — SkillSwap should feel like a knowledgeable friend who introduces you to the right people. Approachable but not juvenile. Professional but never corporate. The interface should inspire confidence that your time exchanging skills will be worthwhile.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Clean & Editorial** — Magazine-quality typography with generous whitespace and a sophisticated grid. Inspired by:
- **Linear**: Speed, polish, and precision in every interaction
- **Airbnb**: Warmth, trust-building through photography and soft surfaces
- **Stripe**: Editorial craft, typography-driven hierarchy, effortless sophistication

**Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards, dark-mode-with-neon "developer tools," glassmorphism card stacks, and cookie-cutter card grids.

### Color Philosophy
Earth-tone palette — **sage** (calm, growth), **terracotta** (energy, warmth), **amber** (optimism, knowledge) — with warm-tinted neutrals. Never pure gray. Dark mode should feel like warm candlelight, not cold void.

### Typography Philosophy
Distinctive serif display font (Instrument Serif) for headings — editorial warmth and character. Geist Sans for body text — clean, modern, highly readable. Dramatic scale jumps between heading levels create clear visual hierarchy and editorial rhythm.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth over sterility** — Every neutral is tinted warm. Every interaction feels human, not robotic.
2. **Typography-first hierarchy** — Size, weight, and font create hierarchy before color or decoration.
3. **Breathing room** — Generous spacing between sections, tight grouping within. Visual rhythm over uniform padding.
4. **Purposeful motion** — Animate to provide feedback and guide attention, never to decorate.
5. **Editorial confidence** — Bold type, asymmetric layouts, clear point-of-view. Not timid, not loud.
